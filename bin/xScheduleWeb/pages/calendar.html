      Each night shows the schedules that start that evening, with sunrise and sunset based times worked out for that date. Pick a night to see what plays on the lights, minute by minute.

      When schedules overlap, the higher priority one plays and the other waits, shown striped. Two schedules with the same priority that overlap are a clash: the one that started first keeps playing. Give one of them a higher priority to choose.

      The Season check lists the nights in a range with no show, the clashes, and the nights a schedule skips. Schedules are edited in xSchedule on the PC.
